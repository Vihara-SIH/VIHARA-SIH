# 🌏 Vihara — One Stop Destination, For All Your Destinations

> **AI-powered travel planning and tourism platform designed to simplify the entire travel planning experience.**

Vihara is an intelligent travel and tourism web application that brings **trip planning, personalized itineraries, budgeting, routes, accommodation, travel information, tourist attractions, events, and local recommendations** into one unified platform.

Instead of switching between multiple applications, websites, maps, blogs, and booking platforms, users can use Vihara to plan their journey from a single place.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Planning a trip usually requires users to search across multiple platforms for:

- Places to visit
- Tourist attractions
- Routes and travel times
- Accommodation
- Transportation
- Budget estimation
- Weather information
- Local food and events
- Activities based on personal interests

This creates several problems:

- 🔍 Information is scattered across multiple platforms
- ⏳ Trip planning is time-consuming
- 💰 Difficult to estimate the actual trip budget
- 📍 Difficult to identify nearby and relevant places
- 🗺️ Difficult to optimize routes and travel time
- 👥 Group travel requires additional coordination
- 📅 Difficult to fit all desired places into the available number of days

Vihara addresses these problems by combining these processes into a single intelligent travel platform.

---

# 💡 Our Solution

Vihara uses **AI + real-time APIs + cloud services** to transform a few simple user inputs into a structured travel plan.

The user provides:

1. 📍 Destination
2. 📅 Number of days
3. 👥 Travel type — Solo, Family, Friends, or Couple
4. ❤️ Travel interests/categories
5. 💰 Budget preference

Vihara then processes this information and generates a personalized travel experience including:

- 🗓️ Day-wise itinerary
- 💰 Estimated budget
- 🗺️ Optimized routes
- 📍 Tourist place recommendations
- 🏨 Accommodation information
- 🚗 Travel information
- 🌦️ Weather information
- 🎫 Attraction/event information
- 🃏 Place cards containing useful location information

---

# ⭐ Key Features

## 🧠 AI-Powered Trip Planning

Vihara analyzes the user's destination, duration, travel type, interests, budget, available attractions, distances, and travel times to generate a personalized itinerary.

The AI helps determine:

- Which places should be visited
- Which places can be grouped together
- How much time should be spent at each location
- What should be visited on each day
- How routes can be organized efficiently

---

## 📍 Intelligent Destination Selection

Users can search for destinations using an easy-to-use interface.

Vihara supports:

- Major Indian cities
- Tourist destinations
- Multiple destination selection
- Location-based search
- Place autocomplete

---

## 🏷️ Vihara Category Mapper

Vihara maps destinations to relevant travel categories such as:

- 🛕 Spiritual
- 🏛️ Heritage
- 🌿 Nature
- 🏔️ Adventure

The system can further map locations to appropriate subcategories.

For example:

A coastal destination can provide beach-related recommendations, while an inland destination can automatically avoid irrelevant beach categories.

This helps prevent users from selecting categories that are not applicable to their destination.

---

## 📍 Nearby Places

Vihara identifies attractions and useful places around the selected destination.

Nearby locations can include:

- Tourist attractions
- Restaurants
- Historical places
- Nature spots
- Religious places
- Activities
- Other relevant points of interest

---

## 🗺️ Route Optimization

Vihara uses location coordinates and route information to calculate:

- Distance between places
- Estimated travel time
- Efficient movement between attractions
- Logical ordering of destinations

This information is provided to the AI to help generate a practical itinerary.

---

## 💰 Smart Budget Estimation

Vihara estimates the minimum expected cost of a trip using available travel and accommodation information.

The budget can consider:

- Accommodation
- Transportation
- Attraction costs
- Travel between destinations
- Other relevant expenses

The system can provide:

- 👤 Estimated minimum cost per person
- 👥 Estimated cost for the complete group

This helps users understand whether their planned trip is financially feasible.

---

## 🌦️ Weather-Aware Itinerary

Weather information can be incorporated into trip planning.

The itinerary can take expected weather conditions into account while recommending activities and organizing the schedule.

For example:

- Outdoor activities can be prioritized during suitable weather.
- Indoor attractions can be preferred when weather conditions are unfavorable.

---

## 🏨 Accommodation & Travel Information

Vihara is designed to bring accommodation and transportation information into the same travel-planning workflow.

Users can explore relevant:

- Hotels/accommodations
- Transportation options
- Travel costs
- Travel durations

This reduces the need to switch between different platforms while planning.

---

## 🎫 Events & Local Experiences

Vihara aims to help users discover:

- Local events
- Activities
- Food experiences
- Attractions
- Cultural experiences

This makes the travel experience more than just visiting tourist attractions.
