About this Gathering

As evening descends over Ibrahim Bagh, the sun softens behind the colossal granite domes of the Qutb Shahi Necropolis. In this sacred, centuries-old garden sanctuary, the acclaimed Nizami Brothers Ensemble gathers to rekindle the timeless spiritual harmonies of the 13th-century Sufi poet Hazrat Amir Khusrau and Punjabi mystic Bulleh Shah.

Unlike commercial hall concerts, this mehfil is strictly purely acoustic. Supported solely by authentic harmonium, dholak, tabla, and layered vocal claps, the acoustic resonance carries through the natural soundboards of the Mughal lime-plaster arches. Guests are welcomed with fragrant jasmine gajras and chilled rose-cardamom sherbet, taking their places on traditional handwoven white dastarkhans under open constellations.

Historical Sanctuary

Qutb Shahi Tombs Heritage Park

The Qutb Shahi Tombs represent the finest surviving synthesis of Persian, Hindu, and Deccani architectural traditions in southern India. Extensively conserved under the patronage of the Aga Khan Trust for Culture, this 106-acre necropolis creates an unmatched natural acoustic amphitheatre sheltered from the bustling city.
